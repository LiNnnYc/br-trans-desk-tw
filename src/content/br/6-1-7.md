> **Key usage purposes (as per X.509 v3 key usage field)**

> Private Keys corresponding to Root Certificates MUST NOT be used to sign Certificates except in the following cases:

對應至根憑證（Root Certificate）的私密金鑰**不得（MUST NOT）**用於簽署憑證，但以下情況除外：

> 1. Self-signed Certificates to represent the Root CA itself;

1. 代表根憑證機構（Root CA）本身之自簽憑證；

> 2. Certificates for Subordinate CAs and Cross-Certified Subordinate CA Certificates;

2. 下屬憑證機構（Subordinate CA）之憑證及交互認證之下屬憑證機構憑證（Cross-Certified Subordinate CA Certificate）；

> 3. Certificates for infrastructure purposes (administrative role certificates, internal CA operational device certificates); and

3. 用於基礎設施目的之憑證（行政角色憑證、CA 內部運作裝置憑證）；以及

> 4. Certificates for OCSP Response verification.

4. 用於 OCSP 回應驗證之憑證。
