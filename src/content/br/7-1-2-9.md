> **Precertificate Profile**

> A Precertificate is a signed data structure that can be submitted to a Certificate Transparency log, as defined by [RFC 6962](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc6962). A Precertificate appears structurally identical to a Certificate, with the exception of a special critical poison extension in the `extensions` field, with the OID of 1.3.6.1.4.1.11129.2.4.3.

預憑證（Precertificate）是一種可提交至憑證透明度（Certificate Transparency）日誌的已簽署資料結構，如 RFC 6962 所定義。預憑證在結構上與憑證完全相同，唯一例外是在 `extensions` 欄位中有一個特殊的關鍵毒素（poison）延伸欄位，OID 為 1.3.6.1.4.1.11129.2.4.3。此延伸欄位確保預憑證不會被符合 RFC 5280 的用戶端接受為有效憑證。已簽署預憑證的存在可被視為對應憑證亦已存在之證據，因為該簽章代表 CA 可能簽發此憑證之有約束力的承諾。

> A Precertificate is created after a CA has decided to issue a Certificate, but prior to the actual signing of the Certificate. The CA MAY construct and sign a Precertificate corresponding to the Certificate, for purposes of submitting to Certificate Transparency Logs. The CA MAY use the returned Signed Certificate Timestamps to then alter the Certificate's `extensions` field, adding a Signed Certificate Timestamp List, as defined in [Section 7.1.2.11.3](#712113-signed-certificate-timestamp-list) and as permitted by the relevant profile, prior to signing the Certificate.

預憑證在 CA 決定簽發憑證之後、實際簽署憑證之前建立。CA **得（MAY）**建構並簽署對應於該憑證之預憑證，以提交至憑證透明度日誌。CA **得（MAY）**使用返回的已簽署憑證時間戳記，在簽署憑證前修改憑證之 `extensions` 欄位，新增如第 7.1.2.11.3 節所定義、並經相關剖繪允許之已簽署憑證時間戳記清單。

> Once a Precertificate is signed, relying parties are permitted to treat this as a binding commitment from the CA of the intent to issue a corresponding Certificate, or more commonly, that a corresponding Certificate exists. A Certificate is said to be corresponding to a Precertificate based upon the value of the `tbsCertificate` contents, as transformed by the process defined in [RFC 6962, Section 3.2](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc6962#section-3.2).

預憑證一經簽署，信賴憑證者得視其為 CA 有意簽發對應憑證之有約束力承諾，或更常見的情況是，視其為對應憑證已存在之證明。憑證是否對應於某預憑證，係根據 `tbsCertificate` 內容的值，依 RFC 6962 第 3.2 節所定義之流程轉換後加以判定。

> This profile describes the transformations that are permitted to a Certificate to construct a Precertificate. CAs MUST NOT issue a Precertificate unless they are willing to issue a corresponding Certificate, regardless of whether they have done so. Similarly, a CA MUST NOT issue a Precertificate unless the corresponding Certificate conforms to these Baseline Requirements, regardless of whether the CA signs the corresponding Certificate.

本剖繪描述對憑證所允許之轉換，以建構預憑證。CA **不得（MUST NOT）**簽發預憑證，除非其願意簽發對應憑證，無論其是否已簽發。同樣地，CA **不得（MUST NOT）**簽發預憑證，除非對應憑證符合本文件要求，無論 CA 是否簽署對應憑證。

> A Precertificate may be issued either directly by the Issuing CA or, when issued prior to 2026-03-15, by a Technically Constrained Precertificate Signing CA, as defined in [Section 7.1.2.4](#7124-technically-constrained-precertificate-signing-ca-certificate-profile). If issued by a Precertificate Signing CA, then in addition to the precertificate poison and signed certificate timestamp list extensions, the Precertificate issuer field and, if present, `authorityKeyIdentifier` extension, may differ from the Certificate, as described below.

預憑證得直接由簽發憑證機構簽發，或於 2026-03-15 之前由技術受限預憑證簽署 CA（如第 7.1.2.4 節所定義）代為簽發。若由預憑證簽署 CA 簽發，則除預憑證毒素及已簽署憑證時間戳記清單延伸欄位外，預憑證之 issuer 欄位及 `authorityKeyIdentifier` 延伸欄位（若存在）可能與憑證有所不同，如下所述。

**注意**：本剖繪要求預憑證之 `serialNumber` 欄位與對應憑證完全相同。RFC 5280 第 4.1.2.2 節要求憑證之 `serialNumber` 須唯一。就本文件而言，預憑證不被視為受該要求規範之「憑證」，因此得與對應憑證具有相同之 `serialNumber`。然而，這並不允許兩個預憑證共用相同之 `serialNumber`，除非它們對應於同一份憑證，否則將表示存在兩份共用相同 `serialNumber` 的對應憑證。
