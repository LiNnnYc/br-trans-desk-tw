> **Training Requirements and Procedures**

> The CA SHALL provide all personnel performing information verification duties with skills-training that covers basic Public Key Infrastructure knowledge, authentication and vetting policies and procedures (including the CA's Certificate Policy and/or Certification Practice Statement), common threats to the information verification process (including phishing and other social engineering tactics), and these Requirements.

憑證機構**應（SHALL）**為所有執行資訊驗證職責之人員提供技能訓練，內容涵蓋基本公開金鑰基礎建設（Public Key Infrastructure，PKI）知識、鑑別（Authentication）與審查政策及程序（包括憑證機構之憑證政策（Certificate Policy，CP）及／或憑證業務作業基準（Certification Practice Statement，CPS））、資訊驗證程序中的常見威脅（包括網路釣魚及其他社交工程手法），以及本文件要求。

> The CA SHALL maintain records of such training and ensure that personnel entrusted with Validation Specialist duties maintain a skill level that enables them to perform such duties satisfactorily.

憑證機構**應（SHALL）**保存此類訓練記錄，並確保受委以驗證專員（Validation Specialist）職責之人員維持足以勝任此等職責之技能水準。

> The CA SHALL document that each Validation Specialist possesses the skills required by a task before allowing the Validation Specialist to perform that task.

憑證機構**應（SHALL）**在允許驗證專員執行某項任務前，記錄該驗證專員具備執行該任務所需之技能。

> The CA SHALL require all Validation Specialists to pass an examination provided by the CA on the information verification requirements outlined in these Requirements.

憑證機構**應（SHALL）**要求所有驗證專員通過憑證機構就本文件要求中所載資訊驗證要求所提供之測驗。
