> **Root CA Authority Key Identifier**

> | **Field**                   | **Description** |
> | ---                         | ------- |
> | `keyIdentifier`             | MUST be present. MUST be identical to the `subjectKeyIdentifier` field. |
> | `authorityCertIssuer`       | MUST NOT be present |
> | `authorityCertSerialNumber` | MUST NOT be present |

| **欄位** | **說明** |
| --- | ------- |
| `keyIdentifier` | **應（MUST）**存在。**應（MUST）**與 `subjectKeyIdentifier` 欄位完全相同。 |
| `authorityCertIssuer` | **不得（MUST NOT）**存在 |
| `authorityCertSerialNumber` | **不得（MUST NOT）**存在 |
