> **Identity**

> If the Subject Identity Information is to include the name or address of an organization, the CA SHALL verify the identity and address of the organization and that the address is the Applicant's address of existence or operation. The CA SHALL verify the identity and address of the Applicant using documentation provided by, or through communication with, at least one of the following:

若主體身分資訊（Subject Identity Information）擬包含組織之名稱或地址，CA **應（SHALL）**驗證該組織之身分與地址，且該地址為申請者（Applicant）之存在或營運地址。CA **應（SHALL）**透過以下至少 1 項方式所提供之文件或通訊，驗證申請者之身分與地址：

> 1. A government agency in the jurisdiction of the Applicant's legal creation, existence, or recognition;

1. 申請者法律設立、存在或經認可之司法管轄區內的政府機構；

> 2. A third party database that is periodically updated and considered a Reliable Data Source;

2. 定期更新且被視為可靠資料來源（Reliable Data Source）之第三方資料庫；

> 3. A site visit by the CA or a third party who is acting as an agent for the CA; or

3. 由 CA 或擔任 CA 代理人之第三方進行實地訪查；或

> 4. An Attestation Letter.

4. 證明信函（Attestation Letter）。

> The CA MAY use the same documentation or communication described in 1 through 4 above to verify both the Applicant's identity and address.

CA **得（MAY）**使用上述第 1 至 4 項所述之相同文件或通訊方式，同時驗證申請者之身分與地址。

> Alternatively, the CA MAY verify the address of the Applicant (but not the identity of the Applicant) using a utility bill, bank statement, credit card statement, government-issued tax document, or other form of identification that the CA determines to be reliable.

或者，CA **得（MAY）**使用公用事業帳單、銀行對帳單、信用卡對帳單、政府簽發之稅務文件，或其他經 CA 認定為可靠之身分證明形式，驗證申請者之地址（但非申請者之身分）。
