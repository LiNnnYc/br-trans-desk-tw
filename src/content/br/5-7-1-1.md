> **Incident Response and Disaster Recovery Plans**

> CA organizations shall have an Incident Response Plan and a Disaster Recovery Plan.

各憑證機構組織**應（shall）**具備事故應變計畫及災難復原計畫。

> The CA SHALL document a business continuity and disaster recovery procedures designed to notify and reasonably protect Application Software Suppliers, Subscribers, and Relying Parties in the event of a disaster, security compromise, or business failure. The CA is not required to publicly disclose its business continuity plans but SHALL make its business continuity plan and security plans available to the CA's auditors upon request. The CA SHALL annually test, review, and update these procedures.

憑證機構（Certification Authority，CA）**應（SHALL）**記錄業務持續性與災難復原程序，旨在於災難、安全洩漏或業務失敗之事件中，通知並合理保護應用軟體供應商（Application Software Supplier）、用戶（Subscriber）及信賴憑證者（Relying Party）。憑證機構不被要求公開揭露其業務持續性計畫，但**應（SHALL）**在稽核人要求時向憑證機構之稽核人提供其業務持續性計畫及安全計畫。憑證機構**應（SHALL）**每年測試、審查及更新這些程序。

> The business continuity plan MUST include:

業務持續性計畫**應（MUST）**包括：

> 1. The conditions for activating the plan,

1. 啟動計畫之條件；

> 2. Emergency procedures,

2. 緊急程序；

> 3. Fallback procedures,

3. 退備程序；

> 4. Resumption procedures,

4. 恢復程序；

> 5. A maintenance schedule for the plan;

5. 計畫之維護排程；

> 6. Awareness and education requirements;

6. 認知與教育要求；

> 7. The responsibilities of the individuals;

7. 個人之職責；

> 8. Recovery time objective (RTO);

8. 復原時間目標（Recovery Time Objective，RTO）；

> 9. Regular testing of contingency plans.

9. 緊急計畫之定期測試。

> 10. The CA's plan to maintain or restore the CA's business operations in a timely manner following interruption to or failure of critical business processes

10. 憑證機構在關鍵業務程序中斷或失敗後，及時維護或恢復憑證機構業務運作的計畫；

> 11. A requirement to store critical cryptographic materials (i.e., secure cryptographic device and activation materials) at an alternate location;

11. 要求在備用地點儲存關鍵密碼材料（即安全密碼裝置及啟動材料）；

> 12. What constitutes an acceptable system outage and recovery time

12. 可接受的系統停機及復原時間之定義；

> 13. How frequently backup copies of essential business information and software are taken;

13. 基本業務資訊及軟體備份副本的備份頻率；

> 14. The distance of recovery facilities to the CA's main site; and

14. 復原設施與憑證機構主要場地之距離；以及

> 15. Procedures for securing its facility to the extent possible during the period of time following a disaster and prior to restoring a secure environment either at the original or a remote site.

15. 在災難發生後至於原始場地或遠端場地恢復安全環境之前，儘可能確保其設施安全的程序。
