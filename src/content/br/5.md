> **MANAGEMENT, OPERATIONAL, AND PHYSICAL CONTROLS**

> The CA/Browser Forum's Network and Certificate System Security Requirements are incorporated by reference as if fully set forth herein.

CA/Browser Forum 的網路與憑證（Certificate）系統安全要求已藉由參照方式併入本文件要求，如同在此完整表述。

> The CA SHALL develop, implement, and maintain a comprehensive security program designed to:

憑證機構（Certification Authority，CA）**應（SHALL）**開發、實施及維護一項全面的安全計畫，旨在：

> 1. Protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of Certificate Data and Certificate Management Processes;

1. 保護憑證資料與憑證管理流程（Certificate Management Processes）的機密性、完整性及可用性；

> 2. Protect against anticipated threats or hazards to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the Certificate Data and Certificate Management Processes;

2. 防範對憑證資料與憑證管理流程之機密性、完整性及可用性所預期的威脅或危害；

> 3. Protect against unauthorized or unlawful access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction of any Certificate Data or Certificate Management Processes;

3. 防範未經授權或非法的存取、使用、揭露、變更或破壞任何憑證資料或憑證管理流程；

> 4. Protect against accidental loss or destruction of, or damage to, any Certificate Data or Certificate Management Processes; and

4. 防範任何憑證資料或憑證管理流程之意外遺失、破壞或損壞；以及

> 5. Comply with all other security requirements applicable to the CA by law.

5. 遵守法律適用於憑證機構之所有其他安全要求。

> The Certificate Management Process MUST include:

憑證管理流程**應（MUST）**包括：

> 1. physical security and environmental controls;

1. 實體安全與環境控制；

> 2. system integrity controls, including configuration management, integrity maintenance of trusted code, and malware detection/prevention;

2. 系統完整性控制，包括組態管理、受信任程式碼之完整性維護，以及惡意軟體偵測／防範；

> 3. network security and firewall management, including port restrictions and IP address filtering;

3. 網路安全（Network Security）與防火牆管理，包括連接埠限制及 IP 位址（IP Address）過濾；

> 4. user management, separate trusted-role assignments, education, awareness, and training; and

4. 使用者管理、獨立的受信任角色（Trusted Role）指派、教育、認知及訓練；以及

> 5. logical access controls, activity logging, and inactivity time-outs to provide individual accountability.

5. 邏輯存取控制、活動紀錄及閒置逾時，以提供個人權責性。

> The CA's security program MUST include an annual Risk Assessment that:

憑證機構之安全計畫**應（MUST）**包括每年的風險評估（Risk Assessment），其：

> 1. Identifies foreseeable internal and external threats that could result in unauthorized access, disclosure, misuse, alteration, or destruction of any Certificate Data or Certificate Management Processes;

1. 識別可預見的內部和外部威脅，該等威脅可能導致未經授權存取、揭露、誤用、變更或破壞任何憑證資料或憑證管理流程；

> 2. Assesses the likelihood and potential damage of these threats, taking into consideration the sensitivity of the Certificate Data and Certificate Management Processes; and

2. 考量憑證資料與憑證管理流程的敏感性，評估這些威脅的可能性與潛在損害；以及

> 3. Assesses the sufficiency of the policies, procedures, information systems, technology, and other arrangements that the CA has in place to counter such threats.

3. 評估憑證機構為應對此類威脅而實施的政策、程序、資訊系統、技術及其他安排的充足性。

> Based on the Risk Assessment, the CA SHALL develop, implement, and maintain a security plan consisting of security procedures, measures, and products designed to achieve the objectives set forth above and to manage and control the risks identified during the Risk Assessment, commensurate with the sensitivity of the Certificate Data and Certificate Management Processes. The security plan MUST include administrative, organizational, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the Certificate Data and Certificate Management Processes. The security plan MUST also take into account then-available technology and the cost of implementing the specific measures, and SHALL implement a reasonable level of security appropriate to the harm that might result from a breach of security and the nature of the data to be protected.

基於風險評估，憑證機構**應（SHALL）**開發、實施及維護一項安全計畫，該計畫由安全程序、措施和產品組成，旨在實現上述目標，並管理和控制在風險評估期間識別之風險，且與憑證資料及憑證管理流程的敏感性相稱。該安全計畫**應（MUST）**包括適合憑證資料及憑證管理流程敏感性的行政、組織、技術和實體保護措施。該安全計畫亦**應（MUST）**考慮當時可用的技術以及實施特定措施的成本，並**應（SHALL）**實施與安全漏洞可能造成的損害及受保護資料之性質相適應的合理安全水準。
