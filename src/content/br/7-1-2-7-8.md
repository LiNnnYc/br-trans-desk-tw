> **Subscriber Certificate Basic Constraints**

> | **Field**           | **Description** |
> | ---                 | ------- |
> | `cA`                | MUST be FALSE |
> | `pathLenConstraint` | MUST NOT be present |

| **欄位** | **說明** |
| --- | ------- |
| `cA` | **應（MUST）**為 FALSE |
| `pathLenConstraint` | **不得（MUST NOT）**存在 |
