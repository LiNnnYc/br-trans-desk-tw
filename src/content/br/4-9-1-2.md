> **Reasons for Revoking a Subordinate CA Certificate**

> The Issuing CA SHALL revoke a Subordinate CA Certificate within seven (7) days if one or more of the following occurs:

於發生下列一項或多項情形時，簽發憑證機構（Issuing CA）**應（SHALL）**於 7 日內廢止下屬憑證機構憑證：

> 1. The Subordinate CA requests revocation in writing;

1. 下屬憑證機構以書面方式請求廢止；

> 2. The Subordinate CA notifies the Issuing CA that the original certificate request was not authorized and does not retroactively grant authorization;

2. 下屬憑證機構通知簽發憑證機構原始憑證請求未經授權，且未溯及既往給予授權；

> 3. The Issuing CA obtains evidence that the Subordinate CA's Private Key corresponding to the Public Key in the Certificate suffered a Key Compromise or no longer complies with the requirements of [Section 6.1.5](#615-key-sizes) and [Section 6.1.6](#616-public-key-parameters-generation-and-quality-checking);

3. 簽發憑證機構取得證據，顯示憑證中公開金鑰（Public Key）所對應之下屬憑證機構私密金鑰（Private Key）遭受金鑰洩漏（Key Compromise），或不再符合 [Section 6.1.5](#615-key-sizes) 與 [Section 6.1.6](#616-public-key-parameters-generation-and-quality-checking) 之要求；

> 4. The Issuing CA obtains evidence that the Certificate was misused;

4. 簽發憑證機構取得證據，顯示憑證遭誤用；

> 5. The Issuing CA is made aware that the Certificate was not issued in accordance with or that Subordinate CA has not complied with this document or the applicable Certificate Policy or Certification Practice Statement;

5. 簽發憑證機構獲悉憑證未符合本文件要求簽發，或下屬憑證機構未遵守本文件要求或適用之憑證政策（Certificate Policy，CP）或憑證業務作業基準（Certification Practice Statement，CPS）；

> 6. The Issuing CA determines that any of the information appearing in the Certificate is inaccurate or misleading;

6. 簽發憑證機構判定憑證中出現之任何資訊不正確或具誤導性；

> 7. The Issuing CA or Subordinate CA ceases operations for any reason and has not made arrangements for another CA to provide revocation support for the Certificate;

7. 簽發憑證機構或下屬憑證機構因任何原因停止營運，且未安排其他憑證機構為該憑證提供廢止支援；

> 8. The Issuing CA's or Subordinate CA's right to issue Certificates under these Requirements expires or is revoked or terminated, unless the Issuing CA has made arrangements to continue maintaining the CRL/OCSP Repository; or

8. 簽發憑證機構或下屬憑證機構依據本文件要求簽發憑證之權利到期、遭廢止或終止，除非簽發憑證機構已做出安排以繼續維護 CRL/OCSP 儲存庫；或

> 9. Revocation is required by the Issuing CA's Certificate Policy and/or Certification Practice Statement.

9. 簽發憑證機構之憑證政策及／或憑證業務作業基準要求進行廢止。
