> **DBA/Tradename**

> If the Subject Identity Information is to include a DBA or tradename, the CA SHALL verify the Applicant's right to use the DBA/tradename using at least one of the following:

若主體識別資訊（Subject Identity Information）擬包含 DBA 或貿易名稱，CA **應（SHALL）**透過以下至少 1 項方式，驗證申請者（Applicant）使用該 DBA／貿易名稱之權利：

> 1. Documentation provided by, or communication with, a government agency in the jurisdiction of the Applicant's legal creation, existence, or recognition;

1. 申請者法律設立、存在或經認可之司法管轄區內的政府機構所提供之文件或與其進行之通訊；

> 2. A Reliable Data Source;

2. 可靠資料來源（Reliable Data Source）；

> 3. Communication with a government agency responsible for the management of such DBAs or trade names;

3. 與負責管理此類 DBA 或貿易名稱之政府機構進行通訊；

> 4. An Attestation Letter accompanied by documentary support; or

4. 附有證明文件之證明信函（Attestation Letter）；或

> 5. A utility bill, bank statement, credit card statement, government-issued tax document, or other form of identification that the CA determines to be reliable.

5. 公用事業帳單、銀行對帳單、信用卡對帳單、政府簽發之稅務文件，或其他經 CA 認定為可靠之身分證明形式。
