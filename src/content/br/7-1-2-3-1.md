> **Technically Constrained Non-TLS Subordinate CA Extensions**

> | **Extension**                     | **Presence**    | **Critical**          | **Description** |
> | ---                              | --               | --                     | --- |
> | `authorityKeyIdentifier`          | MUST            | N                     | See [Section 7.1.2.11.1](#712111-authority-key-identifier) |
> | `basicConstraints`                | MUST            | Y                     | See [Section 7.1.2.10.4](#712104-ca-certificate-basic-constraints) |
> | `crlDistributionPoints`           | MUST            | N                     | See [Section 7.1.2.11.2](#712112-crl-distribution-points) |
> | `keyUsage`                        | MUST            | Y                     | See [Section 7.1.2.10.7](#712107-ca-certificate-key-usage) |
> | `subjectKeyIdentifier`            | MUST            | N                     | See [Section 7.1.2.11.4](#712114-subject-key-identifier) |
> | `extKeyUsage`                     | MUST[^eku_ca]   | N                     | See [Section 7.1.2.3.3](#71233-technically-constrained-non-tls-subordinate-ca-extended-key-usage)|
> | `authorityInformationAccess`      | SHOULD          | N                     | See [Section 7.1.2.10.3](#712103-ca-certificate-authority-information-access) |
> | `certificatePolicies`             | MAY             | N                     | See [Section 7.1.2.3.2](#71232-technically-constrained-non-tls-subordinate-ca-certificate-policies) |
> | `nameConstraints`                 | MAY             | \*[^name_constraints] | See [Section 7.1.2.10.8](#712108-ca-certificate-name-constraints) |
> | Signed Certificate Timestamp List | MAY             | N                     | See [Section 7.1.2.11.3](#712113-signed-certificate-timestamp-list) |
> | Any other extension               | NOT RECOMMENDED | -                     | See [Section 7.1.2.11.5](#712115-other-extensions) |

| **擴充欄位** | **出現要求** | **關鍵** | **說明** |
| --- | -- | -- | --- |
| `authorityKeyIdentifier` | 應（MUST） | N | 參見[第 7.1.2.11.1 節](#712111-authority-key-identifier) |
| `basicConstraints` | 應（MUST） | Y | 參見[第 7.1.2.10.4 節](#712104-ca-certificate-basic-constraints) |
| `crlDistributionPoints` | 應（MUST） | N | 參見[第 7.1.2.11.2 節](#712112-crl-distribution-points) |
| `keyUsage` | 應（MUST） | Y | 參見[第 7.1.2.10.7 節](#712107-ca-certificate-key-usage) |
| `subjectKeyIdentifier` | 應（MUST） | N | 參見[第 7.1.2.11.4 節](#712114-subject-key-identifier) |
| `extKeyUsage` | 應（MUST）[^eku_ca] | N | 參見[第 7.1.2.3.3 節](#71233-technically-constrained-non-tls-subordinate-ca-extended-key-usage) |
| `authorityInformationAccess` | 宜（SHOULD） | N | 參見[第 7.1.2.10.3 節](#712103-ca-certificate-authority-information-access) |
| `certificatePolicies` | 得（MAY） | N | 參見[第 7.1.2.3.2 節](#71232-technically-constrained-non-tls-subordinate-ca-certificate-policies) |
| `nameConstraints` | 得（MAY） | \*[^name_constraints] | 參見[第 7.1.2.10.8 節](#712108-ca-certificate-name-constraints) |
| Signed Certificate Timestamp List | 得（MAY） | N | 參見[第 7.1.2.11.3 節](#712113-signed-certificate-timestamp-list) |
| 任何其他擴充欄位 | 不建議（NOT RECOMMENDED） | - | 參見[第 7.1.2.11.5 節](#712115-other-extensions) |

[^eku_ca]: 雖然 RFC 5280 第 4.2.1.12 節指出此擴充欄位通常僅出現於終端實體憑證，但本文件要求使用此擴充欄位，藉由限縮 CA 憑證範圍以進一步保護信賴憑證者，此做法已由多家應用軟體供應商實作。

[^name_constraints]: 有關此擴充欄位之進一步要求（含關鍵性設定），參見[第 7.1.2.10.8 節](#712108-ca-certificate-name-constraints)。
