> **OCSP Responder Validity**

> | **Field**   | **Minimum**                          | **Maximum** |
> | --          | ----                                 | ----        |
> | `notBefore` | One day prior to the time of signing | The time of signing |
> | `notAfter`  | The time of signing                  | Unspecified |

| **欄位** | **最小值** | **最大值** |
| -- | ---- | ---- |
| `notBefore` | 簽署時間前一日 | 簽署時間 |
| `notAfter` | 簽署時間 | 未指定 |
