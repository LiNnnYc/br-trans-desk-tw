> **Data Source Accuracy**

> Prior to using any data source as a Reliable Data Source, the CA SHALL evaluate the source for its reliability, accuracy, and resistance to alteration or falsification. The CA SHOULD consider the following during its evaluation:

在使用任何資料來源作為可靠資料來源（Reliable Data Source）之前，憑證機構（Certification Authority，CA）**應（SHALL）**評估該來源的可靠性、準確性以及防篡改或防偽造的能力。CA 在評估期間**宜（SHOULD）**考慮以下事項：

> 1. The age of the information provided,

1. 所提供資訊的時效，

> 2. The frequency of updates to the information source,

2. 資訊來源的更新頻率，

> 3. The data provider and purpose of the data collection,

3. 資料提供者及資料收集的目的，

> 4. The public accessibility of the data availability, and

4. 資料取得的公開可接近性，以及

> 5. The relative difficulty in falsifying or altering the data.

5. 偽造或篡改資料的相對難度。

> Databases maintained by the CA, its owner, or its affiliated companies do not qualify as a Reliable Data Source if the primary purpose of the database is to collect information for the purpose of fulfilling the validation requirements under this [Section 3.2](#32-initial-identity-validation).

由 CA、其所有者或其關係企業（Affiliate）維護的資料庫，若該資料庫的主要目的是為了收集資訊以滿足本 [Section 3.2](#32-initial-identity-validation) 下的驗證要求，則不符合可靠資料來源的資格。
